## 2. Standard UTM Tracking Taxonomy

Every marketing channel uses the unified TRUSTGRID 95-column telemetry format:

- **`utm_source`**: `linkedin`, `youtube`, `email`, `newsletter`, `whatsapp`, `twitter`
- **`utm_medium`**: `post`, `description`, `pinned_comment`, `signature`, `broadcast`, `profile_link`, `inmail`
- **`utm_campaign`**: e.g., `gpu_optimization_20pct`, `ai_agentic_swarm`, `november_2026_q4`
- **`utm_content`**: specific variation (e.g., `cta_quick_session`, `cta_chatbot`, `header_button`)

All pages capture these parameters upon arrival, persist them in `sessionStorage` and `localStorage`, and attach them to every lead dispatched to **Supabase**, **Google Sheets**, and **Jira**.

## 4. Verification and Live Telemetry

Whenever a user visits any of these URLs or submits an inquiry:
1. **Google Analytics 4 / Google Tag Manager** records the session with exact `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign`.
2. **Microsoft Clarity** captures session heatmaps and interaction recordings.
3. **TRUSTGRID 95-Column Behavioral Telemetry Engine** streams forensics to Supabase and Google Sheet #2.
4. **Lead Dispatch Service** sends lead records to:
   - Supabase `leads` table
   - Google Sheet #1
   - Jira Workspace (DFX project)
   - Real-time Email notifications
